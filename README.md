# Shopora Sample Corporate Website

This is a complete single-page sample website for Shopora.

## Replace before publishing
- Legal company name
- Headquarters address
- Founding year
- Countries/markets actually served
- Company history and leadership
- Customer support email
- Business partnership email
- Social links
- Terms, Privacy and Refund policy URLs
- All sample claims
- Placeholder headquarters images
- Mock Shopora screenshots

## Run locally
Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or use any static web server.

## Recommended next step
Replace the placeholder visual cards with real Shopora headquarters photos and screenshots, then connect the navigation/policy links to your real pages.
